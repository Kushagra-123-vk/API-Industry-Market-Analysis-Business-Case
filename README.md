# Strategic Infrastructure & API Market Analytics
### Strategy & Business Internship | Jakson Group

## Overview

This repository consolidates strategic, financial, and analytical work undertaken across two large-scale infrastructure projects (~₹4,000 Cr combined) and an API manufacturing business case.

The project integrates market research, SQL analytics, KPI dashboards, competitive benchmarking, value engineering, and government tender monitoring to support executive decision-making and long-term capital allocation.

---

## Business Problems

### 1. Infrastructure Projects
- Improve project delivery timelines.
- Monitor government tender execution.
- Reduce cost overruns through value engineering.
- Track contractor performance and capital deployment.
- Build executive KPI dashboards.

### 2. API Manufacturing Venture
- Assess market attractiveness and growth opportunities.
- Benchmark competitors and EBITDA performance.
- Evaluate CRDMO and export opportunities.
- Analyze policy incentives and supply-chain localization.
- Develop an investment thesis for market entry.

## Key Contributions

### Infrastructure Analytics
- Supported KPI design and reporting across two infrastructure projects valued at **₹2,000 Cr each**.
- Improved government tender adherence, achieving **95% on-time execution**.

### API Market Intelligence

- Analyzed EBITDA margins, market share, and growth trajectories.
